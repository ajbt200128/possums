## Gates that are currently CLOSED

1. **Platform retention of accounting owners:** this feature branch's local owner-safe shutdown/readiness source has passed independent review; [scoped evidence](blue-green-verification.md#authorized-local-runtime-implementation--accepted-local-scope) records the tested boundary. That code is not deployed or platform-qualified. Promotion must demonstrably retain the old enclave until accepted handlers/preflight/accounting owners finish; socket count zero, ordinary SIGTERM or a fixed grace delay is not proof. Successful local drain does not prove all Hyper-owned upload buffers disappeared, nor preserve sessions or balances. No automatic uncertain-generation replay.
2. **Client cutover recovery / active-run affinity:** local main has explicit-submit expiry renewal, but valid cached trust/auth does not unconditionally rediscover a deployment. The selected additional plan is [bounded recovery after eligible pre-inference auth/key failure](blue-green-runtime-plan.md#packet-e--bounded-pi-deployment-recovery-before-inference): changed public attestation hash is only a hint; full verification/auth precede resumed setup. The originating explicit run and its native tool continuations share at most three recovery attempts with bounded cancellable backoff, preserving the conversation/completed tool results without rerunning tools. Fully verified trust may change between model requests, never during an already-dispatched generation. This does not migrate old gateway sessions or guarantee every subsequent request succeeds; unconditional continuity still needs stronger supported endpoint affinity/control. Drain alone cannot preserve full sessions; no fallback to arbitrary keys/releases.
3. **Overlapping ledgers:** `AppState::new` constructs `Accounting::new(auth.account_budgets())`; provisioned demo budgets initialize separately in every enclave. Auth, submission, idempotency and concurrency state are memory-only too. A held candidate with the same paid secrets can duplicate spendable demo credit even before promotion if credentials are used at its review endpoint. Do not authenticate or infer against it. Preservation of secret references is not preservation of balances. No safe overlapping paid accounting protocol exists in this packet.
4. **Review verifier compatibility:** existing `clients/pi/bootstrap.ts` discovers latest at the fixed production origin; `qualifyPublished` and `Channel.published` bind to `PUBLISHER.origin`. Do not simply replace an acquisition URL or use a caller-supplied verified flag. A review endpoint with a port needs a separately reviewed, public-only explicit-tag verifier entry point that reuses the pinned JS libraries and exact authority checks; it must not construct an authenticated client. Native CLI v3 has the unresolved platform-signer mismatch; neither adoption nor bypass is approved. The Go helper's separate v3 result does not qualify the review endpoint. Legacy-v2 freshness limitations remain.
5. **Discovery coordination:** candidate artifacts must be published without moving paid latest from the currently serving release. Inspect the pinned measurement action's actual latest behavior first; the existing workflow does not explicitly enforce this. Do not dispatch it unchanged on the assumption latest stays put. Latest is discovery, not authority. Promotion and latest publication are not one atomic operation: the gap must fail closed before credentials. Coordinate a maintenance/fresh-session window or prove a separate safe mechanism before claiming uninterrupted admission.

These gates prevent a zero-session-loss claim and currently prevent an approved paid overlap rollout. A ready candidate and matching measurements alone do not open them. The target sequence and interruption boundaries are in the [runtime plan](blue-green-runtime-plan.md#complete-target-cutover-and-user-experience). Local server/readiness source is independently accepted; [Pi qualification](blue-green-pi-qualification.md) confirmed native/nested ambiguity at the tested boundary, so recovery activation remains blocked. Operational actions remain held.

## Offline checklist lint (safe to adopt now)

`scripts/plan-held-update.py` reads at most 16 KiB of JSON from stdin. It performs no network/platform/crypto operation and emits no command. It is **not a Tinfoil response decoder, verifier, approval, or promotion gate**. Success means only that a manually normalized checklist is internally consistent; `promote_allowed` is always false and all substantive blockers remain. Do not feed raw platform output or secrets into it.

Exact local input schema (no other fields):

- `expected`, `observed`: objects with `tag` (stable `vX.Y.Z`), `manifest_sha256`, `measurement_sha256`, `hpke_key_sha256` (lowercase 64-digit hex). These are operator-authored public comparisons, not proof. Measurement digest means SHA-256 of the chosen public measurement representation, identically encoded on both sides; key digest means SHA-256 of the endorsed public HPKE key bytes. A future real verifier must perform its existing native comparisons, not substitute this lint.

- `strategy`: exactly `blue_green`; `review_state`: exactly `held_ready`, manually normalized only after inspecting supported metadata. Unknown/missing/replacement state rejects.
