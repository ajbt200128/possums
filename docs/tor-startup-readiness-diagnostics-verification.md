## Narrow contract

This fills only the gap after successful local launch/initialization and before first SDK Running. It reuses the **same** `status_events()` consumed by `engine_status`; no second client, stream decoder, SDK logger, peer/request hook or probe is added. Original startup ownership, nine-record limit and transition metrics are unchanged. The observer is created only on the opt-in Tor serving path. Disabled telemetry short-circuits before credential lookup and constructs no transport/TLS/HTTP work; platform stderr is separate.

One bounded watch cell holds closed onion state/problem categories and a latched first terminal with coarse elapsed bucket. At most two readiness records: waiting at 30 seconds if pending, then first-local-ready, observed engine failure or unconfirmed at 180 seconds. Earlier readiness/failure yields only a terminal. SDK Runtime/DescriptorUpload/Ipt map by variant, never by formatting their contents. Pinned features omit Pow; non-exhaustive/feature additions map to unknown. Status-stream EOF/panic retain distinct closed codes. No raw SDK value enters the cell or encoder.

Elapsed time uses Tokio's monotonic Instant (saturating elapsed): under 30s, 30s to under 60s, 60s to under 180s, or 180s and above. No exact duration/time fields, duration metrics, new metric families/dimensions, dataset or identifiers are added. Existing milestone timestamps remain available separately. An observed Running is historical local SDK evidence, **not current health, verified serving, remote reachability or an attestation/publication guarantee**.

Delivery uses existing official OTLP types, fixed resource/scope, normal Honeycomb US routing/configuration/off switch and the existing bounded transport/response classification. One owned at-most-one-second best-effort log attempt per readiness record; no retries, spool or detached task. The OTLP budget remains two readiness records. An additional **at most one local-only readiness-delivery warning** preserves the first observed configuration/sender failure's closed constraint; it is never exported and adds no metric/event export family. Intentional off (`Ok(None)`) produces no warning. No HTTP status is invented: the existing sender does not preserve it. Absent ingestion means unavailable, not successful submission or health. No new metrics are sent by this observer.

The diagnostic upload future is selected alongside the existing serving/drain future, not awaited inside engine/source polling or readiness updates. A terminal local attempt is committed **synchronously at observation**, before empty serving/drain can complete in that same poll. Waiting/deadline and observation share the bounded watch-cell serialization: no duplicate local terminal and no waiting after terminal. Linux independently reopens only a validated stderr FIFO with nonblocking flags, formats at most 1024 bytes, and attempts one atomic write. Full/unopenable/unsupported output is dropped without taking the global stderr lock, changing shared flags, using a blocking fallback or spawning a writer. Darwin/files/TTYs are explicitly unsupported for this local path. This is best-effort capture, not a platform-delivery guarantee. Finishing serving still drops the separate upload future and owned I/O. The 180-second deadline ends observation only; a final delivery attempt may take one further second. It does not stop Tor, change readiness, restart, cancel, infer billing or replay. First Running cannot be overwritten by Broken during stalled waiting delivery. Subsequent engine faults retain the original serving/drain policy without further readiness logs. Connection permits, bridge pairs, JoinSet ownership, upstream closure, wire contracts and health predicates are unchanged.
